1888 was the last year of the Yukon Gold Rush, and the first year after the Great Die Up blizzards. In other words, it was sunset for the great American cowboy.

Greetings, stranger.

Welcome to my very own slice of the internet. What else can truly belong to me?

There's this joke people make about engineers and techies, which concludes that the more you know about technology and the internet, the less you want anything to do with it - I've had a disdain for big tech for about as long as I can remember, and yet I find myself working in the tech industry today. I hope that coding here, with HTML, CSS, JS, as if it's 2009 will be my respite. Contradictions make us human.

This website is a major WIP. Eventually, if you poke around enough, you'll learn a lot about me.
