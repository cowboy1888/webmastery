The Webmaster

You got me. My name is not actually Coneja [/koˈnexa/] - it means rabbit in Spanish!


I'm a chronic tea drinker and a computer science student who is quite passionate about Latin American + African history, 90s-00s videogames, older operating systems, and low-level programming. In other words, I tend towards that which is out of date.


I have yet to fully detangle why that is, but I suspect it's out of disdain for big tech. It's a sort of looming raincloud I've become uncomfortably familiar with after a lifetime of poking around online.
